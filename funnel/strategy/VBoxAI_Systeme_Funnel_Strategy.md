# VBox AI — Systeme.io Funnel Strategy
**Platform:** Systeme.io | **Design:** Custom HTML (dark purple glowing theme)
**Last updated:** October 2026

---

## Funnel Overview

The goal is a simple 3-product pipeline that warms a cold real estate agent lead into a Brand Foundation buyer, then offers natural upgrades.

```
[Opt-in Page]
      ↓
[Thank You / Bridge Page]
      ↓
[Sales Page — Brand Foundation Package]
      ↓
[Checkout (Systeme.io native)]
      ↓
[Upsell 1 — Marketing Suite]
      ↓  (decline → Downsell)
[Upsell 2 — Website Building]
      ↓
[Welcome / Onboarding Page]
```

---

## Page-by-Page Breakdown

---

### PAGE 1 — Opt-in / Lead Capture

**Systeme.io Page Type:** Opt-in page  
**URL slug:** `/free-checklist` (or similar)

**Purpose:** Capture email via a free lead magnet. Filter in agents who feel their online presence is lacking.

**Lead Magnet Options (pick one):**
- *"The Real Estate Agent Online Presence Checklist"* — 10-point self-audit of their profiles, bios, and visibility
- *"The 5 Things Stopping Buyers From Finding You Online"* — short PDF guide
- *"Your First 30 Days Online — The Agent Setup Roadmap"* — quick-start checklist

**Recommended:** The checklist — it creates awareness of the exact gap VBox AI fills.

**Headline:**
> "Is Your Online Presence Costing You Clients?"

**Sub-headline:**
> Grab the free checklist every real estate agent needs before they update another profile.

**Form fields:** First Name + Email  
**Button CTA:** "Send Me the Checklist →"  
**Below button (micro-copy):** *No spam. Just the good stuff.*

**Custom HTML section:** Full-page opt-in with hero area + form embed code from Systeme.io

---

### PAGE 2 — Thank You / Bridge Page

**Systeme.io Page Type:** Thank you page (after opt-in)  
**URL slug:** `/checklist-on-the-way`

**Purpose:** Confirm delivery, warm them up emotionally, introduce VBox AI as the next logical step. This is a soft sales bridge — not a hard pitch.

**Structure:**
1. **Confirmation block:** "Your checklist is on its way to [email]."
2. **Empathy paragraph:** Acknowledge the real pain — agents are busy, brand setup falls to the bottom of the list
3. **Introduce VBox AI:** One short paragraph — what it is, who it's for, what it does
4. **Soft CTA:** "While you wait — see how agents are getting this done without a VA"  
   → Button links to the Sales Page

**Headline:**  
> "It's on its way. And here's what agents do next."

**Body copy direction:**  
*Most agents download this checklist, scan it, and realize they have work to do — but no idea when they'll get to it. That's where VBox AI comes in. We take the whole thing off your plate, build it correctly the first time, and hand you finished files ready to paste.*

**CTA Button:** "See How VBox AI Works →"

---

### PAGE 3 — Sales Page (Brand Foundation Package)

**Systeme.io Page Type:** Sales page  
**URL slug:** `/brand-foundation`

**Purpose:** Convert warm leads into Brand Foundation buyers.

**Page Structure:**

#### Hero Section
- **Headline:** "Your brand, done for you."
- **Sub-headline:** "The complete AI-powered setup for real estate agents who are too busy building their business to build their brand."
- **CTA:** "Get the Brand Foundation Package →"

#### Problem / Empathy Section
- Headline: "You know you need this. You just don't have time for it."
- 3 pain points (icon + 1-line each):
  - Your Zillow bio still says "I'm passionate about real estate"
  - Your LinkedIn profile and your website say two different things
  - You've been meaning to update your Google Business Profile for 6 months

#### Solution Section
- Headline: "One package. Everything set up. Nothing left to figure out."
- How it works (3 steps):
  1. Complete the intake form (15–20 min)
  2. VBox AI builds your verified profile, bios, and brand guide
  3. You get labeled, ready-to-paste files — no tech skills needed

#### What's Included Section
- Verified Professional Profile (your source of truth)
- Master Bio + 5 platform-ready bios (Zillow, Realtor.com, LinkedIn, Google, Instagram)
- Email signature
- Elevator pitch
- Brand Foundation Guide (brand story, voice, values, visual direction, content pillars)
- Local Visibility Audit (what's inconsistent, what's missing, what to fix first)
- Platform implementation checklist

#### Compliance / Trust Section
- "Fair Housing compliant by design"
- "AI-verified — not just generated, reviewed"
- "Built on your real information, not templates"

#### Pricing Section
- Package name + price
- "What you get" summary list
- Primary CTA button: "Get Started →"

#### FAQ Section (5–7 questions)
- How long does it take?
- Do I need any tech skills?
- What if my information changes?
- Is this compliant with Fair Housing?
- What happens after I pay?

#### Closing CTA
- Headline: "Ready to check this off your list?"
- Button: "Get the Brand Foundation Package →"

### PAGE 4 — Checkout

**Systeme.io Page Type:** Order page (Systeme.io native checkout)  
**No custom HTML needed** — use Systeme.io's built-in checkout with your branding applied.

**Order bump (optional):**  
Add a low-ticket add-on at checkout — e.g., a *Content Starter Pack* (5 branded post captions + 1 email template) for a small flat fee. Impulse buy at checkout, easy to deliver.

---

### PAGE 5 — Upsell 1: Real Estate Marketing Suite

**Systeme.io Page Type:** Upsell page (one-click)  
**URL slug:** `/upgrade-marketing`

**Purpose:** Offer the Marketing Suite immediately after purchase, while the buyer is in a "yes" mindset.

**Structure:**
- **Congratulations block:** "Your Brand Foundation is confirmed! Before you go…"
- **Transition line:** "Now that your brand identity is set — here's how to put it to work."
- **Offer headline:** "Add the Marketing Suite — and show up professionally from day one."
- **What's included:** Listing Presentation · Open House Kit · Social Media Asset Pack · Email Templates
- **Why now:** "Everything is built to match your brand — this is the fastest way to get client-ready materials."
- **Two buttons:**
  - "Yes — Add the Marketing Suite" (primary, glowing purple)
  - "No thanks, I'll come back to this later" (text link, decline)

---

### PAGE 6 — Downsell (Optional, after Upsell 1 decline)

**Systeme.io Page Type:** Downsell page  
**URL slug:** `/marketing-lite`

**Purpose:** Capture buyers who declined the full Marketing Suite with a smaller offer.

**Offer:** Just the *Social Media Asset Pack* or just the *Listing Presentation Template* at a lower price point.

**Headline:** "Just the essentials — at a smaller investment."

---

### PAGE 7 — Upsell 2: Website Building

**Systeme.io Page Type:** Upsell page  
**URL slug:** `/upgrade-website`

**Purpose:** Offer the Website Building service to complete their online presence.

**Structure:**
- **Transition line:** "One more thing — your brand deserves a home."
- **Offer headline:** "Add a professional website — built around everything we just created."
- **Why it's easy now:** "Because your bios, brand story, and visual direction are already done — your website practically builds itself from here."
- **What's included:** Homepage · About Page · Buyer & Seller Pages · Local SEO Foundation · Compliance-ready disclosures
- **Two buttons:**
  - "Yes — Build My Website" (primary)
  - "No thanks" (text link)

---

### PAGE 8 — Welcome / Onboarding Thank You

**Systeme.io Page Type:** Thank you page (after purchase)  
**URL slug:** `/welcome`

**Purpose:** Orient the new client, set expectations, hand off the intake form.

**Structure:**
1. **Welcome block:** "Welcome to VBox AI. You just made a great call."
2. **What happens next (numbered list):**
   - Step 1: Check your email — your confirmation and intake form link are there
   - Step 2: Complete the intake form at your own pace (15–20 min)
   - Step 3: VBox AI gets to work — you'll receive your completed files within [X business days]
   - Step 4: Review, approve, and paste — your implementation checklist walks you through every platform
3. **Intake form button:** "Start Your Intake Form →" (links to form)
4. **Support line:** "Questions? Email us at hello@vbox-ai.com"

## Email Automation Sequence (Systeme.io)

| Trigger | Email | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Opt-in | Lead magnet delivery | Immediate |
| Opt-in | "Did you check your list?" soft pitch | Day 2 |
| Opt-in | Social proof / story email | Day 4 |
| Opt-in | "Here's what's included" | Day 6 |
| Opt-in | Final CTA | Day 8 |
| Purchase | Welcome + intake form link | Immediate |
| Purchase | Reminder: intake form | Day 2 (if not submitted) |
| Purchase | Delivery confirmation | On file delivery |
| Purchase | "What's next" follow-up | Day after delivery |
