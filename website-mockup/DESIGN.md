## Overview
A premium, reassuring AI-service identity for busy real estate agents. The system uses an ink-black canvas, disciplined electric-purple highlights, restrained cyan punctuation, and crisp editorial spacing.

## Color usage
Use #080B14 for page backgrounds and #111522 for glass surfaces. Electric purple #8B5CF6 is the primary action and focus color; #22D3EE appears only as a small supporting signal. Text is white for headings and #A7B0C0 for secondary content.

## Typography
Inter carries the full interface. Hero headlines are bold, compact, and high contrast; body copy stays readable and calm. Uppercase micro-labels use generous tracking.

## Layout
Desktop screens use a 12-column feeling with 80px outer gutters, 1200px maximum content width, generous 64–96px section spacing, and restrained density. Glass cards use semi-transparent dark fills, 1px white borders, and selective glow.

## Do's and Don'ts
- Do: use purple bloom behind one focal element per screen.
- Do: keep calls to action obvious and copy reassuring.
- Do: use grid texture as subtle atmosphere, never foreground decoration.
- Don't: place gradients across entire sections when a localized glow will work.
- Don't: use multiple competing accent colors or decorative blobs.
- Don't: overuse shadows; reserve luminous depth for primary actions and featured cards.
